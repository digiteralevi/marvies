Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our lounge appointments, tattoo safety, and pamper packages.

Can I book a tattoo session and a massage on the same day?
Yes! Many of our clients schedule a relaxing body massage or hair spa treatment prior to getting their fine line tattoo. We recommend receiving massage bodywork before your ink session to keep the freshly tattooed area protected.
How sterile and safe are your fine line tattoo services?
Kurt Zara maintains strict hospital-grade cleanliness standards. We use single-use, pre-sterilized needle cartridges opened directly in front of you, along with medical barriers and sterilized workspace equipment for every client.
What makes Marvie's Signature Massage unique?
Marvie combines deep tissue Swedish strokes, pressure point Shiatsu techniques, and comforting traditional Hilot. This therapeutic fusion releases chronic muscle tension in your shoulders and back while promoting overall body relaxation.
How do I know which hair color or treatment is best for my hair?
Our salon specialists perform a quick strand analysis before starting any color, balayage, or straightening treatment. We assess your hair porosity and history to recommend the safest option, such as Keratin smoothing or silk rebonding.
Do I need to book in advance or do you accept walk-ins?
While we accept walk-ins based on daily slot availability, we strongly recommend reserving your session online in advance—especially for package combinations and fine line tattoo consultations—to guarantee dedicated artisan time.
